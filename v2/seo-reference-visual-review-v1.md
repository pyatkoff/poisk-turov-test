# SEO2 visual review checklist

Reference pages must be reviewed at 375, 430, 768, 1024 and 1440 px.

Check:
- hero hierarchy and line wrapping;
- one dominant lead editorial card;
- secondary editorial card balance;
- offer cards at 3/2/1 columns;
- CTA prominence and mobile width;
- related-link wrapping;
- no horizontal overflow;
- shared AnyTour header/footer consistency.

This checklist does not alter indexation or publication state.
